### sparassis_crispa — Sparassis crépu

> Cauliflower fungi (Sparassis crispa): a large rounded clump, like a sponge or a head of
> lettuce, made of many thin flat wavy ribbon-like lobes tightly crisped and folded, cream to
> pale buff with slightly darker tan edges, rising from a hidden thick fleshy base; no cap, no
> stem, no gills. The small one is a younger, tighter, paler clump. Both grow against the
> exposed reddish-brown root and flaky bark at the foot of a pine trunk, just visible at the
> back edge, with a few dry pine needles on the moss.
