### hygrophorus_marzuolus — Hygrophore de mars

> March mushrooms (Hygrophorus marzuolus): stocky and fleshy, irregular lumpy cap, dark slate
> grey to almost black marbled with whitish and grey-brown patches, smooth and dry, margin
> inrolled; thick waxy widely spaced gills, white turning pale grey; short thick white stem
> greying toward the top. The small one in front is a young dome-shaped button, pale silvery
> grey, half pushing up through the litter. A few short flat silver fir needles scattered on
> the moss.
