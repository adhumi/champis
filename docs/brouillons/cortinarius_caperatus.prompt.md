### cortinarius_caperatus — Pholiote ridée

> Gypsy mushrooms (Cortinarius caperatus): convex to bell-shaped cap with a broad blunt boss, straw-yellow to warm
> ochre, radially wrinkled toward the margin and frosted at the centre with a fine whitish, faintly lilac bloom; crowded
> pale clay gills turning rusty brown, with paler finely toothed edges; tall cream-white stem, slightly swollen at the
> base, bearing a distinct persistent white membranous ring, finely grooved on its upper side, in its upper third. The
> small one is a young specimen with a rounded, lilac-frosted cap whose margin is still joined to the stem by the white
> veil. A few bilberry leaves and dry spruce needles on the moss.
